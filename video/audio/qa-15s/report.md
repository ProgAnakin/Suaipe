## Warnings / caveats

- WARNING: cue type 'letter-tick' has at least one instance with < 3 dB cut-through (2.2 dB)
- Not verifiable here: how it *sounds* (taste, harshness, fatigue). Everything was judged from measurements and spectrograms; a listen on a phone speaker, headphones and monitors is still recommended before publishing.
- The ffmpeg used for metering is an old static build (2018); its ebur128 agrees with an independent BS.1770 implementation (own meter row above) to within 0.05 LU.
- Music vs SFX balance: music stem active RMS -23.8 dBFS (stem scale, sum peaking at -1 dBFS); the loudest SFX peak is -4.2 dBFS (20 dB above the music RMS) and the typical UI transient (tap, tick, key, pop ...) peaks at -14.2 dBFS (10 dB above the music RMS). The brief's 'music roughly 18-20 dB below the SFX peaks' holds against the big hits; literally 18-20 dB under every UI sound would make -14 LUFS unreachable, so UI sounds are kept clear by spectral placement and baked ducking instead.
