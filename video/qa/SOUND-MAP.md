# The sound of every event of the picture

250 things happen in the picture that deserve a sound; **242** have one of their own, the other 8 are covered by the sound next to them and say why. Each row below groups the events of one kind; *class* is the loudness class the sound is levelled to (see `qa/SOUND-AUDIT.md`):
**signature** the film's few big moments · **highlight** an arrival or a confirmation · **action** something the viewer does or reads · **movement** air that follows the picture · **texture** keystrokes, ticks, small pops · **whisper** the quietest touches.

Generated by `scripts/qa/sound-map.py` from `audio/picture-events.json` and `audio/cues.json`.
